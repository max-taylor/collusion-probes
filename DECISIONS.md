Why: in pilot 1 (7B, 10 pairs, cues without a number) the innocent cue matched no-cue prices (mean 1.66 vs 1.66) and only 1/10 pairs passed the band and gap filter. - 2026-10-24 10:36: headline-layer tie-break added: when several layers share the best mean CV accuracy, the middle one of them is the headline (`np.isclose` to the max). Why: on seen wordings, layers 1–28 all hit about 100% CV, and `argmax` picked layer 1 by default. Set before running leave-one-wording-out or any baseline.
- 2026-10-25 00:53: Extension 1 changes after C pilot 1 (10 pairs, seed 3, kept as `data/pilotC_*`, excluded). (1) C histories now use 50 rounds and my prices uniform in 1.20–2.20 (rival copies within the same range), instead of 20 rounds in 1.47–1.92. Why: pilot 1 showed no reaction to following (mean price 1.62 follow vs 1.63 shuffle, gap −0.013, 95% CI [−0.078, +0.059]; mean history profit 28.7 vs 28.8). This is the widening the C pilot row allows. (2) C primary test now uses the probe logit (`decision_function`) instead of P(collusion). Why: the probe saturates; every C run and every out-of-sample B run scored 0.000, so a difference was unmeasurable. Same pass rule (bootstrap CI of the follow minus shuffle gap entirely above 0). New pilot is `data/pilotC2_*`, same seed 3.
- 2026-10-25 01:14: Extension 1 C history redesign after C pilot 2 (`data/pilotC2_*`, 10 pairs, excluded). My prices are now held for 3–6 rounds (uniform) at levels drawn from 1.20–2.20, and the rival copies my previous price exactly (noise ±0.02 removed); 50 rounds as before. Why: pilot 2 still showed no reaction (price gap follow minus shuffle −0.027, 95% CI [−0.144, +0.083]; score gap +0.18 logits, CI [−2.2, +2.8]). With i.i.d. own prices the rival was always one jump out of phase, so following never showed the "hold high, rival stays high" pattern; sticky prices also match the live-agent histories Fish et al. use. Cost: C histories differ further from A/B (length, range, structure), so only within-twin C-follow vs C-shuffle comparisons are valid. Side effect: follow twins have higher history profit (e.g. 28.1 vs 26.6 on one history), since matching is rewarded; report mean profit for both twins. New pilot is `data/pilotC3_*`, seed 3.
